## Community Etiquette, Trading Guidelines, and Safety Protocols

Because any large gaming hub attracts bad actors, protecting your account and in-game assets is paramount. The community adheres to strict trading rules designed to prevent scams, spoofing, and unauthorized real-money transactions. 

Never click unknown links sent via direct message (DM), even if the sender claims to be a server administrator or bot. Legitimate moderators will never ask for your account password, email verification codes, or two-factor authentication tokens. Always follow the official [Discord Safety Guidelines](https://discord.com/safety) to maintain optimal privacy and security across your account settings.

### Recommended Safety Checklist for Trading

1. **Verify In-Game Usernames**: Cross-reference the Discord handle with the in-game character name posted in the server's vouch log.
2. **Never Leave the Platform**: Reject any requests to finalize deals via external websites, third-party messaging apps, or payment processors.
3. **Use Pinned Value Sheets**: Check community price charts to avoid heavily lopsided exchanges or exploitative trades.
4. **Document Every Trade**: Record your screen or take clear screenshots of trade agreements and in-game confirmation windows.

| Trading Practice | Safe Protocol | Unsafe Behavior / Red Flag |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initiating Deals** | Post publicly in `#companion-trading` | Unsolicited DMs offering "too good to be true" offers |
| **Trade Execution** | Direct mutual exchange in the official client | Multi-step trades requiring item drops on open maps |
| **Reputation Checking** | Reviewing historical vouch threads inside the hub | Trusting newly created Discord accounts with no history |
| **Compensation** | In-game currency and companion trades | Requests for gift cards, crypto, or cash app transfers |
| **Dispute Resolution** | Opening a ticket via the server support bot | Publicly flaming or attempting counter-scams |

## Frequently Asked Questions About Wonderpaws Discord

### How do I find the verified wonderpaws discord invite link?
The safest way to join is through official sources such as the game's launcher, official website footer, or verified Steam community page. Avoid clicking third-party forum links or search engine ads, as these may redirect to unverified clone servers designed to harvest credentials.

### Is voice chat mandatory when joining team lobbies?
No, voice communication is entirely optional. While voice channels are available for high-tier dungeon coordination, the community maintains active text-based team finder channels (`#lfg-text`) where players communicate efficiently using in-game pings and casual chat.

### What should I do if someone tries to scam me inside the server?
Take clear screenshots of the conversation, note the user's unique Discord snowflake ID (right-click user > Copy User ID), and open a moderation ticket using the server support bot. Never retaliate or post witch-hunting messages in public channels, as this violates server rules.

### Can I share my own Wonderpaws stream or YouTube guides in the channels?
Most community hubs feature a dedicated `#community-creations` or `#content-creators` channel. Be sure to check the specific self-promotion guidelines before sharing links, as posting personal content in `#general-chat` or `#builds-and-tips` is typically prohibited.
