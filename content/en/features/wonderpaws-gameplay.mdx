
## Frequently Asked Questions About Wonderpaws Gameplay

### How does stamina management work in Wonderpaws gameplay?
In **wonderpaws gameplay**, both the player character and active animal companions share an intertwined stamina economy. Players expend energy by swinging tools, sprinting, and clearing obstacles, while companions expend energy when executing special field interactions or puzzle abilities. Stamina can be replenished throughout the day by resting on benches, consuming cooked meals, or using thermal hot springs found across various wilderness maps.

### Can you play Wonderpaws solo, or is multiplayer required?
The full campaign and all sanctuary features are completely playable as a solo experience. However, optional cooperative features allow friends to visit your sanctuary, trade rare companion breeding patterns, and tackle high-tier exploration challenges together without compromising single-player story progression.

### What happens if a companion runs out of energy during an expedition?
If a companion's energy drops to zero during a wilderness run, they enter an "Exhausted" state. While in this state, they cannot cast field abilities or clear hazards, and their movement speed slows down significantly. Exhaustion does not result in permanent loss or penalties, but you will need to guide them back to a shelter or feed them an emergency restorative treat to restore their readiness.

### How do seasonal cycles affect farming and exploration?
The calendar system features four distinct seasonal shifts that dramatically influence gameplay. Certain fish, foraging plants, and companion species only emerge during specific seasons. Furthermore, seasonal weather conditions—such as winter freezes or autumn windstorms—open access to frozen waterways or blow away debris barriers, revealing time-limited exploration areas.
