## Daily Care Routines and Habitat Customization

A companion's willingness to assist you in the field relies heavily on their current morale meter. Neglecting daily grooming or leaving a companion in an unsheltered pen during harsh weather causes their energy output to drop. When morale falls into the red zone, companions may take frequent naps instead of completing their assigned homestead tasks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I unlock my first companion in Wonderpaws?
Your initial companion is introduced during the introductory prologue quests. As you repair the old sanctuary gates, an injured wild creature will visit your camp. By offering it clean water, a soft patch of straw, and basic forage berries, you will complete the introductory bonding tutorial and officially welcome your very first companion to your homestead.

### Can wonderpaws animal friends run away if neglected?
No, companions will never permanently abandon your homestead or disappear from your sanctuary. If you neglect grooming, feeding, or affection for multiple days, their morale meter will simply empty, causing them to sleep more frequently and refuse field expeditions until you spend time rehabilitating their happiness.

### How many companions can follow me during field exploration?
Under standard gameplay rules, you can travel with one primary companion by your side for direct exploration maneuvers and riding. However, upon reaching Homestead Level 4, you unlock the Field Sachel upgrade, allowing a secondary smaller companion (such as a pocket-sized rodent or fledgling bird) to accompany you as a passive scout.

### What is the fastest way to max out affection with wonderpaws animal friends?
The most reliable method to maximize affection is maintaining a strict daily routine: greet your companion each morning, offer their single most preferred gourmet meal, engage in a daily mini-game play session, and clear daily field exploration routes together. Stacking these activities consistently yields maximum Luminary friendship status within two to three in-game weeks.
