### Performance Optimization Tips

To achieve the best balance between battery life, temperature, and visual clarity on PC:

- **Cap Your Frame Rate:** Setting a cap at 60 FPS (or 40 FPS on handheld devices) keeps hardware temperatures low and prevents sudden frametime spikes during weather transitions.
- **Adjust Volumetric Fog:** Lowering volumetric fog from Ultra to High grants a noticeable 10% to 15% frame rate boost in swamp and mountain biomes without noticeable visual degradation.
- **Enable Dynamic Resolution:** If targeting higher display resolutions, using integrated upscaling allows you to maintain crisp text while preserving smooth exploration sequences.
- **Install on Solid-State Storage:** Fast loading drives reduce texture pop-in when fast-traveling between forest sanctuaries.
