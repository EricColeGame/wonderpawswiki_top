## Frequently Asked Questions About Wonderpaws Pets

### What are the best early-game wonderpaws pets for solo players?
For solo beginners, catching a **Sylph Fox** in the Verdant Canopy or a **Frost Panda** in mountainous foothills provides the safest path forward. The Sylph Fox offers passive health restoration that saves valuable healing supplies, while the Frost Panda absorbs heavy physical hits that might otherwise overwhelm lightly armored adventurers.

### Can wonderpaws pets permanently die in combat?
No, companions do not suffer permanent death under standard game rules. If a companion's health drops to zero during an engagement, it enters a temporary knocked-out state for the remainder of the battle. You can revive your partner using a soothing smelling salt or by resting at any unlocked campsite.

### How many wonderpaws pets can you take into battle at the same time?
Players can generally summon one active primary companion for combat and exploration encounters. However, unlocking advanced party management perks later in the campaign allows you to assign a secondary "passive guardian" who provides stat auras and utility perks without physically engaging enemies on the field.

### How do I increase the inventory bag space of my companion?
You can upgrade companion storage capacity by investing talent points into the Utility skill tree or by equipping specialized saddlebags crafted by high-level leatherworkers. Expanding this storage lets your pet carry extra ores, herbs, and equipment during long wilderness expeditions.
