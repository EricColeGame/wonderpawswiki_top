### Woodland Foragers

Woodland Foragers form the cornerstone of your early-game companion roster. These warm-blooded mammals excel at locating forageable goods tucked beneath fallen logs or hidden in tall grass. Because of their calm temperament, they acclimatize quickly to domestic life.

Keeping Woodland Foragers contented requires moderate foliage coverage, plenty of soft bedding, and constant access to clean water troughs. When roaming alongside you, they instinctively highlight edible flora and rare herbs on your navigation radar.

### Celestial Avians and Mystical Burrowers

High-tier companions such as Celestial Avians and Mystical Burrowers present specialized care curves. Avians require elevated perches and open-air aviaries to prevent stress build-up. In return, their keen eyesight can uncover distant points of interest and upcoming weather anomalies before storms roll in.

Conversely, Burrowers prefer dim lighting, soft peat floors, and secluded resting tunnels. When properly bonded, they unveil concealed passages and subterranean treasure troves that players cannot reach on foot.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Wonderpaws Animals

### How many wonderpaws animals can you house in a single sanctuary?
Sanctuary capacity scales directly with your reserve level. Early-game reserves support up to four companions, but expanding your territory through biome permits and clearing natural debris allows you to accommodate dozens across multiple specialized enclosures.

### Can different animal families share the same habitat?
Yes, certain species exhibit mutualistic behaviors when housed together. Woodland Foragers and small Celestial Avians thrive in shared forest pens, as birds help alert foragers to fallen fruit. However, keep territorial burrowers separate from energetic swimmers to prevent stress penalties.

### What are the easiest wonderpaws animals to tame for beginners?
Woodland Foragers, particularly forest hares and soft-furred squirrels, are the easiest companions for new players. They possess modest diet requirements, calm reactions to approaching rangers, and minimal shelter complexity.

### What happens if an animal companion becomes unhappy?
Neglected companions will not flee your sanctuary, but their productivity drops to zero. They will refuse to accompany you on wilderness expeditions, and their passive bonuses will deactivate until their diet, shelter, and grooming needs are properly restored.
