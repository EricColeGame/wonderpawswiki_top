## Core Archetypes of Wonderpaws Companions

Every pet creature in the world falls into a specialized combat and exploration role. Knowing these archetypes helps you identify gaps in your active party and pick creatures that address your character's natural weaknesses.

The game divides allies into four primary classifications: Vanguards, Mystics, Scouts, and Wardens. While any creature can participate in skirmishes, their archetype dictates base stat scaling, active action frequency, and passive aura bonuses.

### Vanguard (Defensive Brawlers)
Vanguards excel at drawing threat away from fragile ranged adventurers. They boast high health pools, natural physical mitigation, and disruptive knockback attacks. If your build focuses on high-damage spellcasting or precision archery, keeping a sturdy Vanguard in front ensures you have space to cast uninterrupted.

### Mystic (Elemental Casters)
Mystic allies manipulate the elements to inflict status ailments, burn down large packs of minions, and amplify magical damage. They scale exceptionally well with elemental affinity bonuses, making them indispensable during boss encounters where breaking elemental barriers is mandatory.

### Scout (Utility & Gathering Specialists)
Scouts are built for agility, foraging efficiency, and critical strike buffs. While slightly more delicate in sustained brawls, their high movement speed and terrain-traversal skills make world exploration significantly faster. They alert players to concealed resource nodes, rare flora, and ambushes before enemies spot you.

### Warden (Support & Restoration)
Wardens focus on health recovery, barrier generation, and cleansing debuffs. A dependable Warden keeps health bars topped off and neutralizes venom, freeze, and burn statuses that otherwise drain your consumable inventory.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Wonderpaws Companions

### How many wonderpaws companions can I have active at one time?
Players can travel with up to three active **wonderpaws companions** simultaneously in their exploration party. You can swap inactive pets into your party from any established campfire or waystone sanctuary without penalty.

### Can wonderpaws companions permanently faint in combat?
No, there is no permanent defeat for your allies. If an ally's health reaches zero during battle, they enter an exhausted state and become temporarily inactive. You can revive them immediately using specialized smelling salts or simply by clearing the ongoing encounter and resting.

### How do I change my companion's elemental focus?
A creature's base element is fixed upon discovery, but feeding them attuned elemental infusions at max friendship allows them to slot dual-element sub-skills, broadening their utility against diverse enemy rosters.

### Do smaller companions have less health than large ones?
Not necessarily. While size often aligns with archetype themes (large creatures frequently being Vanguards), base health scales strictly based on their archetype, gear quality, and your bonding tier rather than physical character model dimensions.
